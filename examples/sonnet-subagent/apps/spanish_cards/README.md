# SPANISH CARDS

Flashcard quiz: see a Spanish word, reveal the English answer, and score what you knew. Each round deals 12 shuffled cards from a built-in deck of 24; your best score is saved.

## Controls

- SPACE / ENTER: reveal the answer
- y: knew it (+1 right), next card (answer side)
- n: missed (+1 miss), next card (answer side)
- ENTER / RIGHT / l: skip to next card without scoring (answer side)
- LEFT / h: go back one card
- r: reshuffle and restart
- q / ESC: quit
